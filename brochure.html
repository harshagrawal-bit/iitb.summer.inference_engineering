About the WorkshopMLIE 2026

Overview

Modern AI is bottlenecked not by training, but by the cost and latency of serving models at scale. MLIE 2026 is a six-day, lab-driven workshop that introduces the engineering discipline of inference — from first principles to deploying an optimised large-language-model endpoint of your own. Previously held only abroad, it comes to India for the first time. Mornings are lecture-led by the international faculty; afternoons are supervised labs on the C-MInDS A100 GPU cluster; the workshop closes with a competitive optimisation capstone.

Curriculum — six modules

01 · Foundations of Inference

Forward pass, latency vs. throughput, roofline analysis.

02 · Model Compression

INT8/INT4 quantization, pruning, distillation.

03 · GPU & Kernel Optimization

CUDA, memory coalescing, fusion, FlashAttention, Triton.

04 · Serving Systems

Dynamic batching, KV-cache, Triton, vLLM, Ray Serve.

05 · LLM Deployment at Scale

Paged attention, parallelism, speculative decoding.

06 · Production & MLOps

Observability, autoscaling, canary, drift detection.
