A landmark first

The inference-engineering workshop comes to India

Run previously only abroad, this flagship workshop is being hosted in India for the first time — and IIT Bombay is the host.

Bringing together instructors who fly in from Tokyo and the United States alongside IIT Bombay faculty, MLIE 2026 is a deliberately small, intensive and exclusive cohort. Over six days you move from the fundamentals of how a model runs to deploying and optimising a production-grade large-language-model service of your own — on the C-MInDS A100 GPU cluster.

  • Lead instructors from Tokyo, the USA and IIT Bombay

Why attend

Bridging the gap between training a model and shipping it

Most courses stop at model accuracy. MLIE 2026 begins where they end — the engineering discipline of running models reliably, cheaply and fast in production.

Production-grade serving

Build low-latency inference services with batching, KV-caching and autoscaling using Triton, vLLM and Ray Serve.

Make models 10× cheaper

Quantization (INT8/INT4), pruning, distillation and speculative decoding to slash cost and memory without losing accuracy.

Down to the GPU

Understand memory hierarchies, CUDA streams and kernel fusion — and profile a real workload on the C-MInDS A100 cluster.

LLMs in the real world

Deploy and optimise large language models — paged attention, tensor/pipeline parallelism, and cost-aware routing.

Reliability & MLOps

Monitoring, canary rollouts, drift detection and the observability stack that keeps inference healthy at 3 a.m.

World-class faculty

Learn directly from internationally renowned researchers in efficient ML and large-scale systems.

Who should attend

Built for the next generation of ML systems engineers

The workshop is open to participants from all IITs, IISc, IIITs, NITs and recognised partner institutions, and a limited number of international participants. It is ideal for:

B.Tech / M.Tech students (pre-final & final year) PhD scholars in ML / Systems Research staff & project associates Early-career ML engineers Faculty wanting hands-on exposure

Prerequisites: working knowledge of Python and a first course in machine learning. Familiarity with PyTorch and the Linux command line is helpful but not required — a primer is shared before the workshop.

Curriculum at a glance

What you will learn

Six themed modules across six days, each pairing lectures from the international faculty with a supervised lab.

01

Foundations of Inference

The forward pass, latency vs. throughput, roofline analysis, and where time and money actually go.

02

Model Compression

Post-training and quantization-aware INT8/INT4, pruning, knowledge distillation and low-rank methods.

03

GPU & Kernel Optimization

CUDA execution model, memory coalescing, operator fusion, FlashAttention and Triton kernels.

04

Serving Systems

Dynamic batching, KV-cache management, Triton Inference Server, vLLM and Ray Serve in practice.

05

LLM Deployment at Scale

Paged attention, tensor & pipeline parallelism, speculative decoding and cost-aware routing.

06

Production & MLOps

Observability, autoscaling, canary releases, drift monitoring and on-call reliability for ML.
