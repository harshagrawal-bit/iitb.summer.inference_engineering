Registration & Fees

One flat fee of ₹8,000 covers the full three-day programme, GPU lab access and on-campus accommodation. Seats are limited to 90.

The fee

₹8,000 per participant, all-inclusive of the essentials

A single registration fee applies to all participants — students, scholars and faculty alike — and is inclusive of 18% GST.

✓ Included in the fee

  • All lectures, labs & the capstone
  • Individual A100 GPU lab access
  • On-campus accommodation (3 nights, 21–24 June)
  • Course kit & materials
  • High-tea / refreshments during sessions
  • Certificate of completion (CEP, IIT Bombay)

✗ Not included

  • Meals — breakfast, lunch & dinner
  • Travel to and from Mumbai
  • Local transport within the city
  • Personal & incidental expenses

Subsidised dining is available at campus canteens and the hostel mess; pay-as-you-go counters operate at the venue.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is the fee really all-inclusive?
The ₹8,000 fee covers the full programme, GPU lab access, course materials, certificate and on-campus accommodation for three nights. Meals and travel are not included — see the fees section above.
I'm from another IIT / IIIT / NIT. Can I attend?
Yes. The school is open to participants from all IITs, IISc, IIITs, NITs and recognised partner institutions, as well as early-career professionals. Carry a valid institute ID for check-in.
What does accommodation look like?
Twin-sharing rooms in campus guest house / hostel accommodation for the nights of 21, 22 and 23 June, so you can arrive the evening before Day 1 and leave on the morning of 25 June.
Are meals available even though they're not included?
Absolutely. Subsidised meals are available at campus canteens and the hostel mess, and pay-as-you-go food counters operate at the venue throughout the school.
What's the refund / cancellation policy?
Cancellations requested in writing on or before 12 June 2026 are eligible for a refund less processing charges. No refund is possible after that date, but you may nominate a substitute from the same institute.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes — participants who complete the programme and capstone receive a certificate of completion issued under the Continuing Education Programme, IIT Bombay.
Do I need a powerful laptop?
No. All heavy computation runs on the C-MInDS GPU cluster, which you access remotely. Any laptop that can SSH and run a browser is sufficient.
