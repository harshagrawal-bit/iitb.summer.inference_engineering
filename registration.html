Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is the free quiz route still open?
No — the Phase 1 qualifier quiz ran 15–29 May 2026 and has now closed. Top performers were offered fully-funded seats by e-mail (results sent 5 June 2026). To join now, take a limited direct seat at ₹20,000.
What if I don't want to take the quiz?
Take a direct seat. A limited number of direct seats are available at ₹20,000 (incl. GST), first-come, first-served. You get exactly the same six-day programme.
Is the ₹20,000 fee all-inclusive?
It covers the full six-day programme, GPU lab access, course materials, certificate and on-campus accommodation for the nights of 20–26 June. Meals and travel are not included — see the fees section above.
I'm from another IIT / IIIT / NIT, or from abroad. Can I attend?
Yes. The workshop is open to participants from all IITs, IISc, IIITs, NITs and recognised partner institutions, plus a limited number of international participants. Carry a valid institute / photo ID for check-in.
What does the quiz cover?
Fundamentals of machine learning and computer systems — basic ML concepts, Python reasoning, and elementary systems/performance thinking. A short syllabus is shared when quiz registration opens.
What's the refund / cancellation policy for direct seats?
Cancellations requested in writing on or before 12 June 2026 are eligible for a refund less processing charges. No refund is possible after that date, but you may nominate a substitute from the same institute.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes — participants who complete the programme and capstone receive a certificate of completion issued under the Continuing Education Programme, IIT Bombay.
