Speakers & Faculty

Three lead instructors from Tokyo, the United States and IIT Bombay anchor the workshop, supported by IITB faculty and invited international practitioners.

Lead instructors

Two international experts & the India convener

The three lead instructors carry the core curriculum across the six days.

Prof. Song Han

Lead Instructor · United States
Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), Cambridge, USA

A leading figure in efficient deep learning, known for foundational work on model compression, quantization and TinyML. Leads the compression and LLM-deployment tracks.

QuantizationModel CompressionTinyML

Prof. Rio Yokota

Lead Instructor · Tokyo, Japan
Institute of Science Tokyo, Japan

An authority on large-scale GPU computing and high-performance deep learning, with extensive work on training and serving very large models. Leads the GPU and kernel-optimization tracks.

GPU ComputingHPCLarge-scale DL

Prof. Sunita Sarawagi

Convener · India
CSE & C-MInDS, IIT Bombay

A leading machine-learning researcher and the host convener for the India edition. Opens the workshop and leads the foundations module.

Machine LearningDeep Learning
