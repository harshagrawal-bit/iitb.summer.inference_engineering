Venue, Travel & Accommodation

The summer school is held on the IIT Bombay campus at Powai, Mumbai — one of India's most scenic and best-connected institute campuses.

The venue

Victor Menezes Convention Centre, IIT Bombay

Lectures are held in the VMCC auditorium and seminar halls; labs run in the C-MInDS / KReSIT computing facilities, a short walk away.

  • Air-conditioned auditorium with full A/V
  • Dedicated lab rooms with high-speed access to the GPU cluster
  • Walking distance from hostels, guest house and canteens
  • Campus Wi-Fi access provided to all participants

Getting here

How to reach IIT Bombay

The campus is in Powai, north-east Mumbai, well connected by air, rail and road.

By air

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) is about 6–8 km away — roughly 25–40 minutes by taxi depending on traffic.

By rail / metro

Kanjurmarg and Vikhroli (Central line) and Ghatkopar (Central/Metro) are the nearest stations — 4–7 km from campus. Metro Line 6 also serves Powai.

By road

App taxis and autos reach the IIT Bombay Main Gate (Powai) easily. Ask for the "IIT Main Gate, Powai". Limited paid parking is available on campus.

On arrival: report to the registration desk at VMCC (Day 1) or the guest-house reception (if checking in on 21 June). Carry a printed or digital confirmation and a valid institute / photo ID.

Stay

Accommodation is included in your fee

Every registered participant is provided on-campus accommodation at no extra cost — it's part of the ₹8,000 fee.

  • Twin-sharing rooms in campus guest house / hostel accommodation
  • Three nights: 21, 22 and 23 June 2026
  • Check-in from 16:00 on 21 June · check-out by 12:00 on 25 June
  • Walking distance to the venue, labs and canteens

Meals are not included. The hostel mess, campus canteens (e.g. Gulmohar, hostel messes) and venue counters offer affordable meals on a pay-as-you-go basis throughout your stay.

Participants who prefer to arrange their own stay may opt out of accommodation during registration; note that the fee remains ₹8,000 as it bundles the full programme.

Around campus

Food & essentials nearby

On campus

Hostel messes, Gulmohar restaurant, the staff canteen and several cafés serve breakfast through dinner at student-friendly prices.

Powai (5–10 min)

Hiranandani Gardens and Powai Plaza offer a wide range of restaurants, cafés, pharmacies and ATMs just outside the campus.

Weather in June

Expect warm, humid and rainy monsoon weather (26–32 °C). Carry an umbrella / light rain jacket and comfortable footwear.

Everything sorted — just bring yourself

Stay, labs and learning are taken care of on campus. Register to reserve your room and seat together.
