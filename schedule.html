Programme Schedule

Three days, six modules. Mornings are lecture-led; afternoons are hands-on labs on the C-MInDS GPU cluster. All times are IST.

Daily rhythm: Registration & high-tea from 08:30 · Lectures 09:00–13:00 · Lunch break 13:00–14:00 (own arrangement) · Supervised labs 14:00–17:30 · Optional evening clinic 18:00–19:00.

08:30 – 09:00

Registration & welcome high-tea

Collect your kit and lab credentials at the VMCC foyer.

09:00 – 09:30

Inaugural & course overview Keynote

Prof. Sunita Sarawagi · Convener, MLIE 2026

Why inference engineering matters, and how the three days are structured.

09:30 – 11:00

Foundations of model inference

Prof. Preethi Jyothi

The forward pass in detail; latency vs. throughput; tail latency; the cost equation for serving.

11:00 – 11:20

Tea break

11:20 – 13:00

Performance modelling & the roofline

Prof. Purushottam Kulkarni

Compute- vs. memory-bound regimes, arithmetic intensity, and reading a roofline plot for a real model.

13:00 – 14:00

Lunch break own arrangement

Campus canteens, hostel mess and venue counters are open.

14:00 – 17:30

Lab 1 — Profiling a model end to end Hands-on

Lab leads & teaching assistants

Set up your GPU instance, profile a ResNet and a small transformer with the PyTorch profiler and Nsight Systems, and identify the bottlenecks.

18:00 – 19:00

Optional evening clinic

Open office-hours for environment setup and debugging.

09:00 – 10:30

Model compression: quantization

Prof. Abir De

Post-training vs. quantization-aware training; INT8 and INT4; calibration; accuracy–latency trade-offs.

10:30 – 11:00

Pruning & knowledge distillation

Invited speaker · Industry

Structured vs. unstructured pruning and distilling large models into deployable students.

11:00 – 11:20

Tea break

11:20 – 13:00

GPU & kernel optimization

Invited engineer · GPU systems

CUDA execution model, memory coalescing, operator fusion, FlashAttention and writing a Triton kernel.

13:00 – 14:00

Lunch break own arrangement

14:00 – 17:30

Lab 2 — Quantize & accelerate Hands-on

Lab leads & teaching assistants

Quantize a transformer to INT8/INT4, fuse kernels, and benchmark the speed-up and accuracy delta on your A100 instance.

17:30 – 18:00

Fireside chat: inference at industry scale Panel

Practitioners on what breaks in production and how teams are organised around it.

18:00 – 19:00

Optional evening clinic

09:00 – 10:30

Serving systems & dynamic batching

Prof. Purushottam Kulkarni

KV-cache management, continuous batching, Triton Inference Server, vLLM and Ray Serve architecture.

10:30 – 11:00

Deploying LLMs at scale

Invited speaker · LLM serving

Paged attention, tensor & pipeline parallelism, speculative decoding and cost-aware routing.

11:00 – 11:20

Tea break

11:20 – 13:00

Production, MLOps & reliability

Invited engineer · MLOps

Observability, autoscaling, canary releases and drift detection for live inference services.

13:00 – 14:00

Lunch break own arrangement

14:00 – 16:30

Capstone — the inference leaderboard Hands-on

All faculty & teaching assistants

Teams optimise a shared model and compete on a live latency–throughput–cost leaderboard.

16:30 – 17:15

Capstone presentations & judging Showcase

Top teams present their approach; judges score on the combined metric.

17:15 – 18:00

Valedictory & certificate distribution

Convener & organising committee

Prizes, certificates and closing remarks.

Note: the schedule is indicative and may be adjusted. Speaker assignments for invited-industry sessions are confirmed closer to the event and announced by e-mail to registered participants.
